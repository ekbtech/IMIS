# ODPC and DHA evidence checklist

Use this as a discovery checklist, not as a compliance declaration or certification application. Confirm current statutory text, regulations, regulator guidance, scope and submission process with ODPC, DHA and Kenyan legal counsel. Mark requirements not applicable only with a recorded rationale.

## Data protection / ODPC evidence

- [ ] Identify controller(s), processor(s), joint roles, accountable management and DPO/privacy lead.
- [ ] Confirm ODPC registration obligations, thresholds, exemptions, renewals and current process for this facility and processing.
- [ ] Maintain a processing inventory: purpose, lawful basis, health/special-category data, data subjects, recipients, processors and transfers.
- [ ] Complete and approve DPIAs for applicable high-risk processing; track mitigations and review triggers.
- [ ] Approve privacy notices, patient rights request handling, correction, objection/restriction and response timelines.
- [ ] Document retention, archival, legal holds, secure deletion and data-minimization rules.
- [ ] Maintain processor agreements, sub-processor controls, access locations and transfer safeguards.
- [ ] Evidence access controls, staff confidentiality/training, audit monitoring and sensitive-record safeguards.
- [ ] Approve breach detection, assessment, regulator/data-subject notification and incident response processes.
- [ ] Record complaints, access/correction requests, decisions, response dates and escalation.

## Digital Health Agency / Ministry of Health evidence

- [ ] Obtain the currently effective Digital Health Act regulations, standards, certification guidance and official version identifiers.
- [ ] Confirm whether the product, hospital, each module, hosting arrangement and each integration require DHA registration, certification or approval.
- [ ] Obtain official HIE/API specifications, current national data profiles, terminology/identifier requirements and change notices.
- [ ] Confirm authoritative test environment, credentials, onboarding, security controls, conformance tests and production go-live approvals.
- [ ] For every integration, document approved field mappings, data minimization, idempotency, delivery/retry behavior, failure handling, reconciliation evidence and interface-version change control.
- [ ] Document approved facility identity, service scope/licensing and applicable county/government policies.
- [ ] Produce data-flow, architecture, hosting, security, privacy, business-continuity and recovery evidence requested by DHA.
- [ ] Demonstrate API authorization, audit events, access reviews, encryption, backup/restore, incident handling and interoperability tests.
- [ ] Keep test evidence synthetic; use production credentials/data only after explicit authorization.
- [ ] Record DHA correspondence, submission, findings, remediation, approval certificate and validity/renewal dates.

## Evidence register

For every item record: requirement ID, source URL/document/version, provision, applicability rationale, evidence location, owner, status, gaps, due date, reviewer, approval and last review. Do not mark an item complete based on this checklist alone. Current detailed DHA certification requirements and operational technical specifications were not verified in this Phase 1 research.
